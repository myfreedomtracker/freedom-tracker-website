Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

The short version: Freedom Tracker is a desktop app. Your portfolio data stays on your computer, and there is no brokerage login. We don't have user accounts, and we never see your holdings, share counts, income, or balances. This page explains the limited cases where any data is sent or handled at all — public price and dividend lookups, update checks, and license checks.

1. Your portfolio data stays on your device

Everything you enter into Freedom Tracker — your holdings, transactions, dividends, budgets, goals, and scenarios — is stored locally in a file on your own computer. We do not receive a copy of that file. There is no Freedom Tracker account, and there is no brokerage login. If you uninstall the app or delete that file, it is gone from the app; we keep no copy because we never receive one. The app does send limited information to third parties as described below, including ticker symbols for market data and license information for license checks.

2. Market data the app fetches

To show live prices and distribution information, the app requests publicly available market data from third-party providers, including Yahoo Finance and stockanalysis.com. These requests contain the ticker symbols being looked up, or the text you type into ticker search — never your share counts, balances, or other portfolio details. Like any web request, they also include your device's IP address, which those providers handle under their own policies. If the app cannot reach the internet, it skips these lookups and can show last-saved prices.

3. Update checks

On launch, the app may check our public software repository (hosted on GitHub) to see whether a newer version is available. This is a standard web request; like any web request it includes your device's IP address, which is handled by GitHub under their own privacy policy. No personal or financial data from your portfolio is sent.

4. Purchases and licensing

Payments are processed by our payment provider, Lemon Squeezy, which acts as the Merchant of Record for the sale. When you buy a license, Lemon Squeezy collects the information needed to process your payment (such as your name, email, billing details, and country) under their privacy policy. We receive only limited order information — such as your email address, country, and license key — so we can deliver your license and provide support. We never receive or store your full payment card details.

After you buy, the app also checks your license with Lemon Squeezy from time to time when you activate, re-check, or deactivate a computer. Those checks send only your license key, a computer activation ID, and a generic name such as "Freedom Tracker (Windows)" or "Freedom Tracker (Mac)" — never your holdings, share counts, or balances. If the app cannot reach the license server, it warns you and keeps working; it does not lock the app just because you have no internet.

5. Email and support

If you contact us at support@myfreedomtracker.com, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in your message, and we use it only to respond to and resolve your request.

6. This website

This site is hosted on Cloudflare. Like most websites, the hosting provider may process standard technical request logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security and reliability. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, a privacy-focused, cookie-free service, to count page visits. It does not track you across sites. We do not place advertising or sell any information collected through this site.

If you sign up on this site for the free Clear Picture Worksheet or other email updates, MailerLite processes the information you enter in that form (at least your email address) on our behalf so we can send you the worksheet and related product emails. You can unsubscribe at any time. MailerLite handles that information under its own privacy policy.

7. What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your information.
  • We do not show ads or use advertising trackers.
  • We do not collect your holdings, share counts, or balances — that portfolio data stays on your computer.
  • We do not require an account to use the application.

8. Children

Freedom Tracker is intended for adults managing their own investments and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Your choices and rights

Because your portfolio data lives only on your device, you are in direct control of it at all times — you can edit, export, back up, or delete it yourself within the app. For the limited order and support information described above, you may contact us to request access to or deletion of that information, and we will respond consistent with applicable law.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will be reflected on this page.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email support@myfreedomtracker.com.
